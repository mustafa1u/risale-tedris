With His creative command, He brings the earth back to life. By the decree of,

اِنْ كَانَتْ اِلَّا صَيْحَةً وَاحِدَةً فَاِذَاهُمْ جَم۪يعٌ لَدَيْنَا مُحْضَرُونَ

There will be but a single cry, then they shall all be brought nigh unto Us; {[*]: Qur'an, 36:53.} that is, "all men and jinn, with a single cry and command shall be brought to Us and made present at the plain of resurrection." Again, by His command,

The hour shall be but a blinking of the eye, or even closer; {[*]: Qur'an, 16:77.} that is, the bringing about of resurrection and the gathering that follows upon it shall take no longer than the opening and closing of an eye, or even less. Then there is the verse,

Your creation and resurrection is as a single nafs, {[*]: Qur'an, 31:28.} meaning the following: "O men! To create you and to bring you to life, to resurrect and gather you, is as easy for me as bringing one nafs to life; it presents no problem to My power." Acccording to the inner sense of these three verses, Allah will bring all men and jinn, all animals, spirit beings and angels to the field of the Supreme Gathering and the great balance with a single command and with great ease. One concern does not interfere with another.

وَمَٓا اَمْرُ السَّاعَةِ اِلَّا كَلَمْحِ الْبَصَرِ اَوْ هُوَ اَقْرَبُ

The remaining Mysteries, from the third and fourth as far as the thirteenth, have been postponed to another time in a fashion disagreeable to me.
